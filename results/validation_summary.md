# Validation summary

Generated by `scripts/run_validation.py`; every number comes from the `results/tables/validation_*.tsv` tables. Primary analyses use tumours with >= 50 SNVs. Sensitivities: min_snvs 20 and 100, reconstruction cosine >= 0.8 (a quality filter that never uses smoking or MSI status), and the variants named in each row. Median cosine is the median reconstruction cosine of the tumours in the group. Descriptive rows list min/max in their own columns.

## Headline

- **Lung (LUAD), primary.** SBS4 fraction in smokers (current + reformed <=15 y; n=273, median 0.502, IQR 0.3507-0.5879) vs lifelong never-smokers (n=33, median 0.110, IQR 0.00864-0.441): AUC 0.82 (95% CI 0.73-0.90), Mann-Whitney p=2.4e-09. The AUC interval excludes 0.5 in the direction 'smokers higher', which is consistent with higher SBS4 attribution in smokers.
- A minority of reported never-smokers show tobacco-like SBS4 exposure. Possible reasons include misreported smoking history, second-hand smoke, or overlap between SBS4 and similar signatures; this analysis cannot tell these apart. In LUAD at >= 50 SNVs, 11/33 lifelong never-smokers (33%, Wilson 95% CI 20-50%) have SBS4 fraction > 0.25 (a descriptive listing aid, not a test); the upper quartile of their SBS4 fraction is 0.44 and the maximum 0.67. Context: Zhang et al. 2021 (Nat Genet) report no strong tobacco-smoking signatures in never-smoker lung cancers, even with second-hand smoke exposure (see References).
- **Melanoma (SKCM), primary.** Summed SBS7a-d fraction: n=417, median 0.910 (IQR 0.839-0.958); summed SBS7a-d is the single largest component in 395/417 tumours (94.7%) and above 50% in 395/417 (94.7%); these two proportions are secondary measures; the counts coincide because a tumour above 50% is necessarily the largest component and here every largest-component tumour is also above 50%. This is consistent with UV-signature dominance (rule: median summed fraction > 0.5).
- **Colorectal (COAD+READ), primary.** Summed MMR fraction (SBS6+14+15+20+26+44) in MSI (n=80, median 0.601, IQR 0.5039-0.6842) vs MSS (n=458, median 0.087, IQR 0.0362-0.1476): AUC 0.97 (95% CI 0.95-0.99), p=1.4e-41. The AUC interval excludes 0.5 in the direction 'MSI higher', which is consistent with higher mismatch-repair signature attribution in MSI-labelled tumours.
- MSI prevalence by case among cases with an MSI call: colon (COAD) 17.5% (75/428; Wilson 95% CI 14.2-21.4%); rectum (READ) 5.8% (9/155; Wilson 95% CI 3.1-10.7%). MSI is expected to be rarer in rectal than in colon cancer, so a lower READ share is expected. The 10-20% sanity check (TCGA Network 2012, Nature 487:330-337) applies to colon only.

Key limiting and negative findings (same tables):

- Pack-years vs SBS4 fraction in ever-smokers, LUAD: Spearman rho 0.03 (95% CI -0.08 to 0.14), p=0.59, n=321: not consistent with a monotonic dose-response.
- Pack-years vs SBS4 mutation count (NNLS-attributed, added secondary test), LUAD: Spearman rho 0.15 (95% CI 0.04 to 0.25), p=0.0075, n=321: association with SBS4 count, which also tracks total burden.
- Pack-years vs SBS4 fraction in ever-smokers, LUSC: Spearman rho -0.01 (95% CI -0.11 to 0.09), p=0.83, n=404: not consistent with a monotonic dose-response.
- Pack-years vs SBS4 mutation count (NNLS-attributed, added secondary test), LUSC: Spearman rho 0.04 (95% CI -0.06 to 0.13), p=0.47, n=404: not consistent with a monotonic dose-response.
- Context for the two dose-response tests: an exposure fraction is compositional (the fractions sum to 1 across the fitted signatures, so it depends on every other signature) and pack-years are self-reported and noisy, so a weak or absent dose-response was not expected to be a sensitive test. The SBS4 count scales with total mutational burden, so the count association may partly track burden. The count test is an added secondary test with no multiplicity correction, and it was added after the fraction result was seen (at Prithviraj's request).
- LUSC is underpowered (14 lifelong never-smokers at >= 50 SNVs): AUC 0.59 (95% CI 0.40-0.76), p=0.25; reported descriptively.
- MSS tumours are not at zero: median summed MMR fraction 0.087 (IQR 0.0362-0.1476); 22% of MSS tumours have SBS6 > 0; SBS1 vs SBS6 fraction in MSS Spearman rho -0.30. This pattern is consistent with exposure trading between similar signatures (SBS1/SBS6) and the noise floor of NNLS refitting, so some MMR attribution in MSS tumours may not reflect a real mismatch-repair defect.
- READ has only 6 MSI tumours at >= 50 SNVs (vs 129 MSS): AUC 0.94 (95% CI 0.82-1.00) is imprecise.
- D5 check (adjacent SNVs counted as SBS vs excluded), summed SBS7a-d: paired median difference 0.000, largest single-tumour |difference| 0.088 (n=417); the median difference is below the 0.05 reporting threshold (the threshold applies to the median, not to individual tumours).
- 7 MSI-labelled tumours (>= 50 SNVs) have summed MMR fraction below 0.25 (a fixed listing cut, not a test): MSI score 0.220-0.766, total SNVs 96-2781, 1 with SBS10a+10b+28 > 0.5. They are kept in all analyses and listed in validation_colorectal_msi_low_mmr.tsv. Low MMR in these may reflect MSIsensor2 false calls, low mutation counts, or POLE-like/unfitted signatures; no explanation is asserted.
- 22 melanomas (>= 50 SNVs) are not UV-dominated (summed SBS7a-d <= 0.5); they are flagged in validation_melanoma_non_uv.tsv and kept in all analyses.

## Cohort flow and the SNV filter

The SNV-count filter uses only each tumour's SNV total and never a smoking or MSI label (it is label-blind). It is nonetheless strongly correlated with smoking status in LUAD, because lifelong never-smokers have fewer mutations.

LUAD lifelong never-smokers: 69 chosen cases, 63 with >= 20 SNVs (in the fit table), 33 with >= 50, 17 with >= 100. Smokers (current + reformed <=15 y): 287 chosen, 273 (95%) with >= 50 and 245 (85%) with >= 100. Never-smokers retained at >= 50: 48%.

Across the three thresholds the LUAD AUC ranges from 0.72 to 0.88 and every 95% CI lower bound is above 0.5 (yes). The >= 50 SNV row is the primary; the >= 20 and >= 100 rows are sensitivity analyses shown in the same table, and the headline is never switched to whichever threshold looks better.

Never-smoker fits are poorer at the primary setting (median reconstruction cosine 0.84 vs 0.93 in smokers). The effect size changes with the threshold because the filter changes who is compared, not because the test changed. **The primary analysis stays at >= 50 SNVs as pre-specified** (D7); the other thresholds are sensitivity analyses and were not used to choose it.

## Limitations and notes

- **SBS1/SBS6 overlap.** SBS1 and SBS6 (and SBS5/SBS40a) are similar, so NNLS can trade exposure between them. Some SBS6 in MSS tumours may be leakage rather than a real MMR defect; see the SBS6 distribution and SBS1-SBS6 correlation above.
- **MSI labels are bioinformatic** (GDC MSIsensor2, tumour BAM only, 20% score cutoff), not clinical MSI/MMR tests.
- **Subset prevalences are approximate**: read from figure dots (Alexandrov 2020, ED Fig. 5).
- **SBS88 (colibactin), SBS36 and SBS10c/d are not fitted** in colorectal.
- **Fractions are NNLS-attributed**, not observed counts, and depend on the approved signature subset.
- **POLE-like hypermutators** (SBS10a/b/28 dominated) can be MSI-labelled yet score low on summed MMR; they are counted in the POLE table, not excluded.
- **Lower fit quality in never-smokers.** Reconstruction cosine is lower in LUAD never-smokers than in smokers (see the table above), and the >= 50 SNV filter removes many never-smokers; fractions of tumours with few SNVs are noisy.
- **Multiple testing.** There are three pre-specified primary analyses: LUAD SBS4 (never vs smoker), SKCM summed SBS7a-d fraction (a descriptive summary, no test), and COAD+READ summed MMR (MSI vs MSS). Secondary and sensitivity analyses are not independent tests and no multiplicity correction is applied.
- **Refitting, not machine learning.** Signatures are assigned by non-negative least-squares refitting to published COSMIC v3.4 signatures; nothing is trained on COSMIC data.
- **Research and education only; not for diagnosis.**
- Wording: results are described as consistent or not consistent with the expected biology; no causal claim is made.
- Group 1 is the group expected higher; AUC > 0.5 means group 1 tends higher. The rank-biserial correlation is 2*AUC-1. CIs are percentile bootstrap (2000 resamples, fixed seed).
- **D6/D6b filters did nothing on these data.** Across all five cohorts the profile step dropped 0 tri-allelic rows and 0 multi-allelic-position rows (results/tables/profile_drops_summary.tsv), so those rules did not change any count. The dominant drops are non-SNP rows and (default mode) adjacent SNVs.
